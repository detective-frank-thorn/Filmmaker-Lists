Filmmakers’ Favorite Films
==========================

Published favorites, poll selections and films explicitly championed by filmmakers in this repository. Each section identifies the context and source. Films can appear on more than one list.

Wes Anderson reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Anderson#Favorite_films
