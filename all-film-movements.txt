Film movements
==============

Dates are approximate. Directors are representative of each movement, and an association may refer to particular films or phases of a career.

French Impressionist Cinema
===========================

Region: France
Approximate period: 1918–1929

Associated directors
- Abel Gance
- Germaine Dulac
- Jean Epstein
- Marcel L’Herbier

German Expressionism
====================

Region: Germany
Approximate period: 1919–late 1920s

Associated directors
- Robert Wiene
- F. W. Murnau
- Fritz Lang
- Paul Leni

Soviet Montage
==============

Region: Soviet Union
Approximate period: Mid-1920s–early 1930s

Associated directors
- Sergei Eisenstein
- Dziga Vertov
- Vsevolod Pudovkin
- Lev Kuleshov
- Alexander Dovzhenko

British Documentary Movement

======================

Region: India

==================

Region: Hong Kong

Associated directors
